Two Plans, One Dream Trip

Both plans depart from Anand Vihar Terminal (ANVT) and cover the magical hills of North Bengal — Kurseong, Dow Hill, and Darjeeling. The only fork is whether you continue to Guwahati, Assam afterwards or return directly.

Plan A

With Guwahati

~6–7 nights total · Maximum experience

  • All of Plan B included
  • Guwahati sightseeing (2 nights)
  • Brahmaputra sunset cruise
  • Kamakhya Temple
  • Umananda Island boat trip
  • Return on Northeast Express from Guwahati
  • Optional: Pobitora Wildlife Sanctuary
  • Great for first-time NE India visitors
Plan B

Hills Only

~4–5 nights total · Less fatigue

  • Kurseong & Dow Hill deep dive
  • Full Darjeeling day exploration
  • Toy train from Kurseong → Darjeeling
  • Tea garden walks & café hopping
  • Minimal travel days — ideal for parents
  • More time in each hill station
  • Return direct from NJP to ANVT
  • Best if parents tire easily

⚖️ Which Plan Should You Choose?

Choose Plan A (with Guwahati) if your parents are reasonably fit for travel, this is your only North East trip for a few years, and you want the full experience — the Brahmaputra cruise alone is unmissable. The train journey from NJP → Guwahati is overnight and comfortable in 3AC/2AC.

Choose Plan B (hills only) if your parents feel fatigue easily, prefer to spend more time in fewer places, or if the monsoon is particularly heavy that year. The hills are more than enough for a beautiful trip — Darjeeling in June mist is extraordinarily atmospheric.

Our recommendation: Plan A, but pace it generously. Take 3AC at minimum, book an extra rest day in Guwahati, and avoid any rushing.

Plan A

🏆 Plan A — With Guwahati

Total duration: 6–7 nights. Best for families who want the full North East India experience — hills, tea gardens, toy trains, and the mighty Brahmaputra.

Ghaziabad/ANVT → NJP → Kurseong → Darjeeling → NJP → Guwahati → ANVT/Ghaziabad
D0
Departure Night — 10 June
Ghaziabad / Anand Vihar Terminal
Evening
Pack & Prepare

Reach Anand Vihar Terminal (ANVT) or board at Ghaziabad (GZB) station. Train 12506 departs ANVT at 07:40 — so either stay the night near ANVT, or plan the next morning's departure from Ghaziabad (which is an intermediate stop, ~30 min after ANVT).

Tip: Since the train departs at 07:40, most families from Ghaziabad prefer to leave home by 06:30, take an auto to GZB station, and board there instead of ANVT.

D1
Train Day — 11 June
Aboard 12506 Northeast Express · ANVT → NJP
07:40 AM
Depart Anand Vihar / GZB

Board the Northeast Express. Settle into your berths — grab the lower berths for parents. The train passes through Ghaziabad (~08:10), Kanpur, Varanasi, Patna, and into North Bengal. The later section from Katihar onwards becomes scenic.

All Day
Relaxed Train Journey

Read, observe the changing landscape, order from the pantry car, or arrange e-catering (Zomato/IRCTC). The train offers meals, snacks and tea. Pack light snacks for parents.

Next AM
Arrive NJP ~07:40

You wake up in the hills! Step out at New Jalpaiguri. The air will already feel cooler. Head to the prepaid taxi counter outside the station and hire a cab for Kurseong.

D2
Arriving the Hills — 12 June
NJP → Kurseong · Dow Hill · Tea Gardens
08:00 AM
NJP → Kurseong by Cab

~44 km, 1.5 hours. Reserve a sedan from the prepaid counter (₹2,800–₹3,200). The road winds up through forest and tea gardens. Perfect monsoon scene — misty, green, dramatic.

09:30 AM
Check-in, Rest & Breakfast

Freshen up at your hotel after the overnight train. Have a proper breakfast — many hotels serve toast, omelette, and Darjeeling tea. Let parents rest for an hour.

11:00 AM
Dow Hill & Eagle's Crag

Visit the mysterious Dow Hill — walk through the silent forest trail, visit the Victoria Boys School area, and take in the views from Eagle's Crag viewpoint. The mist-covered deodar forest is ethereal in June. Keep the pace leisurely for parents.

01:00 PM
Lunch at Kurseong Diaries Café (4th floor, Amarjeet Hotel)

One of the best cafés in Kurseong — great views of the valley, North Indian food, momos, and excellent Darjeeling tea. Order veg thali or sandwiches.

03:00 PM
Tea Estate Walk

Visit the Makaibari Tea Estate (3 km from town) — one of India's oldest and most celebrated estates. Walk between rows of tea bushes in the rain. You can purchase authentic Makaibari tea here directly.

06:00 PM
Evening: Local Market & Rest

Stroll through Kurseong town, browse local shops. Return to hotel for rest and dinner.

D3
Toy Train to Darjeeling — 13 June
Kurseong → Darjeeling (Toy Train) · Full Darjeeling Day
06:30 AM
Board Toy Train at Kurseong Station

Train 52587 (or the slip coach 02451). The 31 km ride to Darjeeling takes ~2.5 hours through clouds, loops, tea bushes, and tiny mountain villages. Children wave from the trackside. Absolutely magical.

09:00 AM
Arrive Darjeeling

Take a shared or reserved cab to your hotel or the Mall Road area. Freshen up and head for breakfast at one of the famous Darjeeling bakeries.

10:00 AM
Batasia Loop & War Memorial

If the toy train joyride is booked, this is the highlight — the train spirals down the loop with panoramic Kanchenjunga views (clear days) or dramatic cloud views (monsoon). The War Memorial here honours Gorkha soldiers.

11:30 AM
Ghoom Monastery

Visit the 1850 Yiga Choeling Monastery at Ghoom — India's highest railway station area. A serene, ancient Buddhist monastery with gorgeous murals.

01:00 PM
Lunch on Mall Road

Head to Darjeeling's famous Mall Road for lunch. Glenary's Bakery & Café (iconic heritage café) or Sonam's Kitchen for vegetarian-friendly Tibetan and Indian food.

02:30 PM
Happy Valley Tea Estate

One of the closest estates to Darjeeling town — walk through rows of tea bushes, learn about plucking, and taste fresh tea. Very senior-friendly terrain.

04:30 PM
The Mall Road & Chowrastha Square

Browse curio shops, tea shops, and woollen goods. The evening fog rolling over Darjeeling is one of the most beautiful sights in India.

16:10 PM
Return to Kurseong by Toy Train (52588)

Or take a shared cab if parents are tired (~₹80 per person). Arrive Kurseong by ~18:40. Dinner at hotel. Pack luggage for early departure tomorrow.

D4
Kurseong → NJP → Guwahati — 14 June
Check out · Travel to NJP · Board overnight train to Guwahati
07:00 AM
Check Out & Head to NJP

Cab from Kurseong to NJP (~₹2,800). Grab breakfast at a dhaba near NJP or at the hotel before leaving.

At NJP
Board Train to Guwahati

Several trains run NJP → Guwahati (Kamakhya). The Kamrup Express (15959) or Tista-Torsa Express (13141) are good options. Journey is ~5–6 hours. Arrive Guwahati/Kamakhya by afternoon.

Afternoon
Arrive Guwahati · Check In · Rest

Head to your hotel near Paltan Bazaar or Fancy Bazaar. Rest and recover from the hills. Evening stroll along the Brahmaputra ghats (Sukreswar Ghat or Kachari Ghat) to soak in the river atmosphere.

D5
Guwahati Sightseeing — 15 June
Kamakhya · Umananda · Brahmaputra Cruise
07:30 AM
Kamakhya Temple

The most powerful shakti peetha in India, perched atop Nilachal Hill. A profoundly atmospheric experience. Mornings are best (less crowd). Take an auto or cab — ₹150–₹250 one way. The Brahmaputra views from the temple hill are stunning.

10:00 AM
Umananda Island Boat Ride

Ferry from Umananda Ghat to the tiny island in the Brahmaputra with a Shiva temple and a troop of rare golden langurs. Return ferry included. A gentle, beautiful excursion perfect for parents.

12:30 PM
Lunch: Assamese Veg Thali

Try an authentic Assamese thali — dal, bora (rice fritters), khar, pitika (mashed potato/fish), leafy greens, and sweets. Restaurants near Fancy Bazaar offer good vegetarian options.

03:00 PM
Rest at Hotel

Afternoon siesta — very important for parents after morning sightseeing.

05:00 PM
Brahmaputra Sunset Cruise 🌅

The undisputed highlight of Guwahati. Board a river cruise from Uzanbazar/Fancy Bazaar Ghat. The mighty Brahmaputra glows amber at sunset. Ferry services like MV Mahabahu run scheduled evening cruises (~₹600–₹800 per person). Book in advance, especially in monsoon.

Evening
Pan Bazaar Night Walk & Dinner

Guwahati's Pan Bazaar area has excellent shops for Assam silk, local food, and evening activity. Dinner at a restaurant serving Assamese or North Indian food.

D6
Optional: Pobitora Day Trip — 16 June
Wildlife Sanctuary · 1-horned Rhino · ~50 km from Guwahati
Optional Venture: Pobitora Wildlife Sanctuary is home to the highest density of Indian one-horned rhinoceroses in the world, just 50 km from Guwahati. Jeep safaris run morning and afternoon. A hired cab for the day costs ₹1,500–₹2,500. If parents are comfortable, this is unmissable. If not, spend the day at the serene Umiam Lake (also 15 km away) for a boat ride and lakeside relaxation.

Cuisine Guide

Vegetarian Food Guide

This region is very vegetarian-friendly, especially in the hills. North Indian dhabas are plentiful near train stations. Here's what to eat where.

🍽️ Must-Try Dishes

🥟
Veg Momos
Steamed or fried Tibetan dumplings — ubiquitous in the hills. Darjeeling & Kurseong have the best ones.
🍜
Veg Thukpa
Hearty Tibetan noodle soup — perfect for cold, foggy evenings in the hills.
🍵
Darjeeling Tea
The world's finest tea — buy directly from estates. First or second flush for best flavour.
🥐
Glenary's Bakery
Iconic Darjeeling institution. Pastries, sandwiches, egg-less cakes. A must visit on Mall Road.
🍛
Assamese Veg Thali
Dal, pitika (mashed sides), rice, khar (alkali curry), leafy greens, sweets. Earthy, wholesome, unique.
🍩
Sel Roti
Nepali rice doughnut — crispy, slightly sweet, sold at roadside stalls in the hills. Perfect with tea.
🫕
Aloo Dum
Spiced potatoes — a staple hill food available everywhere. Great for breakfast with puri.
🍰
Nepali Thali
Dal-bhat-tarkari with gundruk (fermented greens), papad. Incredibly filling and authentic.

Preparation

Packing & Medical Guide

June is early monsoon in North Bengal. The hills will be lush, green, misty, and occasionally rainy. Guwahati in June is hot and humid.

👕 Clothing

🧥Light fleece jacket / sweater (hills can be 12–18°C at night)
🌂Compact folding umbrella (each person)
🧣Light scarf or stole for parents (hill chill)
👖2–3 pairs of comfortable full-length trousers
👕3–4 light cotton T-shirts (for Guwahati heat)
🧤Light rain poncho or windcheater
🧦Woollen socks (1–2 pairs for cold nights)
👒Light cap or hat for Guwahati outdoor sightseeing

👟 Footwear

👟Comfortable walking shoes with grip (for hills & slippery paths)
🩴Light sandals / flip flops for hotel/Guwahati
⚠️Avoid open sandals on Dow Hill forest trails (leeches in monsoon!)
🧦Leeches tip: Wear full socks tucked into shoes on forest walks
